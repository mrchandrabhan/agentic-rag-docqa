# Campus Pantry Smart-Locker Policy

## Placing an order
Students sign in with their campus single sign-on account and can place one order per day. Each order may contain up to 10 items, and perishable items are limited to 3 per order.

## Pickup window
Orders are packed into a smart locker within 4 hours. The student receives a one-time locker code by email, and the code expires 48 hours after the locker is loaded. Uncollected items are returned to inventory after the code expires.

## Privacy
The pantry does not share order history with instructors or financial aid. Staff dashboards show aggregate counts such as locker utilization and popular items, never individual names.

## Accessibility
Students who cannot use the lockers can request a staffed pickup by replying to the order email. Staffed pickups run Monday to Friday from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m.
